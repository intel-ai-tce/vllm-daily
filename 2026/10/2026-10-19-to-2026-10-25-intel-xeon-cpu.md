## Scope notes

- The Pacific-time window maps to 2026-10-19 07:00 UTC through 2026-10-26 06:59 UTC; activity early on October 26 UTC is included when it occurred on October 25 PDT.
- Native CPU items must affect the CPU execution backend, x86 packaging, Xeon NUMA/distributed behavior, or portable CPU behavior. Host-memory/offload items are intentionally separated and do not imply native Xeon inference.
- Issues #63507 and #62687 were reproduced on Apple ARM, but their validation and PyTorch CPU-generator failures are architecture-independent and apply to Xeon. Issue #63565 was reproduced on Intel Arc, but its OffloadingConnector scheduler logic is device-independent and is retained only in the host-memory section.
- Intel XPU-only items such as issues #63666, #63621, #62781, and #62762 were excluded. AMD/ROCm-only work, including MoRI-IO fixes, ROCm Octave, and RDNA kernels, was excluded even when it mentioned host transfer. Generic CUDA, model, frontend, and scheduler work without a direct CPU or offload path was also excluded.
- Recipes issues #1153 and #1156 and renderer PRs #1162/#1175 were excluded because their concrete cases are GPU/Dynamo or general UI behavior rather than Xeon CPU execution. Recipes issue #1174 is retained only as a host-memory recipe request and is explicitly not treated as Xeon validation.
