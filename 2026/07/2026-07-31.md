**What changed and why**  
Lowered the minimum CUDA capability for ModelOpt FP8 quantization from SM89 to SM80, allowing FP8 checkpoints to load on A100 GPUs. This enables vLLM to use its existing weight-only FP8 execution path with emulation on pre-SM89 hardware, as demonstrated with the NVIDIA-Nemotron-3-Nano-30B-A3B-FP8 model.

FP8 emulation may degrade performance for compute-bound models compared to BF16, and accuracy may slightly decrease (observed -2.73 pp on GSM8K). **Key insights**  
The PR includes a measured tradeoff: FP8 emulation on A100 improved throughput by ~24% but reduced accuracy by ~2.7 pp on GSM8K for the tested model. The change is minimal (one line) but has significant behavioral impact, enabling new hardware support with a documented performance/accuracy tradeoff.
